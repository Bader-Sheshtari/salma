# Salma North Star — Permanent Product Direction

- **Date:** 2026-08-22
- **Product:** Salma — Arabic-first health & treatment news for Kuwait/GCC.
- **Companion document:** [`SALMA_MASTER_ROADMAP.md`](./SALMA_MASTER_ROADMAP.md)

## What this document is

This is Salma's permanent product direction and its set of non-negotiable principles. It describes **what Salma is and how to decide** — not what is currently built.

Every significant future decision — product, editorial, or architectural — must align with this document. If a proposal conflicts with a principle here, the proposal changes, or this document is deliberately and explicitly revised. It is not overridden silently.

This document does **not** track execution status. Status, priorities, verified production state, and postponed items live in the Master Roadmap. North Star = direction and principles. Roadmap = execution and status.

---

## 1. The three primary products

Salma is focused on becoming exceptionally strong across three channels:

1. **Website**
2. **Instagram**
3. **Newsletter**

Each one is a **serious standalone editorial product** — not a distribution copy of another channel. Instagram is not a repost of the website. The newsletter is not an RSS feed of published articles. Each is judged on its own editorial merit.

---

## 2. Product quality standard

Everything Salma produces aims to be:

- Highly useful
- Accurate
- Clean, concise, elegant
- Easy to consume
- Visually and editorially attractive
- Respectful of the reader's time

And free from:

- Filler
- Unnecessary repetition
- Generic or low-value content

**The goal of any Salma output is a compact, valuable, intelligent dose of information.** The benchmark is global-quality editorial and product standards — not local or category-average standards.

---

## 3. Editorial philosophy — "After the News"

Salma answers **three questions**, not one:

1. **What happened?**
2. **What does it mean?**
3. **What is behind / after the news?**

"After the News" is a **core Salma editorial identity principle**, not an optional format.

### Example: a new drug or breakthrough announcement

Do not stop at the announcement. Where appropriate, investigate and explain:

- Is it actually important?
- How strong is the evidence?
- What changes versus today?
- Who benefits?
- What are the limitations?
- When could this realistically matter?
- What does it mean for patients?
- What does it mean for healthcare systems?
- What does it mean for Kuwait/GCC, where relevant?
- Which companies or organizations are involved?
- What happened before this?
- What may happen next?
- Can it be explained through simple examples?

### The depth ladder

Depth scales with the importance of the topic:

**short explanation → deeper analysis → investigative / research material**

Not every story earns the top rung. Important stories must not be left on the bottom one.

## 4. AI-first operating model

The long-term operating goal is **minimum human labor + maximum editorial quality**. These are pursued together; neither is traded away for the other.

### What AI does

AI handles as much repeatable work as can safely be automated:

- Discovery
- Research
- Source analysis
- Evidence analysis
- Topic selection
- Drafting
- Summarization
- Translation
- Adaptation by channel
- Instagram content preparation
- Newsletter curation
- Categorization and metadata
- Performance analysis
- Workflow coordination

### What humans do

Humans focus on the small set of things that genuinely require them:

- Important approvals
- Exceptional editorial judgment
- Sensitive or high-risk decisions
- Strategic decisions

### Anti-patterns to avoid

Do not build workflows that require staff to repeatedly:

- Copy and paste
- Manually categorize
- Fill unnecessary forms
- Rewrite the same information once per channel
- Perform repetitive administrative work

**If a repetitive task can reasonably be automated, prefer automation.**

---

## 5. One content intelligence, three channels

One intelligent editorial engine feeds all three products. **A topic enters the system once.**

The AI system then determines what that topic deserves:

- A website article
- An In Brief
- An After-the-News analysis
- A deeper investigation
- An Instagram carousel
- An Instagram short format
- Newsletter inclusion
- Multiple channels
- No publication at all

Each channel receives content **adapted for that medium**, not copied mechanically between channels. "No publication" is a legitimate and expected outcome.

---

## 6. Per-channel principles

### Website

Website excellence prioritizes:

- Content architecture and sections
- Functionality
- Editorial presentation
- Information density
- Discoverability
- Article usefulness
- Reading experience
- In Brief
- Video
- Research and studies
- Deeper analysis and After-the-News formats
- Comments, search, and sharing where genuinely useful
- Mobile experience

### Instagram

Instagram is a **primary product**. The long-term intent is an AI-driven editorial engine producing high-quality:

- Hooks
- Carousels
- Concise explainers
- After-the-News analysis
- Study explanations
- Important numbers
- Comparisons
- Captions
- Source references

It is **not** merely reposting website headlines.

### Newsletter

The newsletter is a **primary product**, and it is **curated, not a feed**.

Its philosophy: *"Here are the few health stories actually worth your time."*

AI selects and prioritizes the most useful content rather than including everything published. The result must be concise, intelligent, attractive, and editorially valuable.

---

## 7. Branding status — intentionally unsettled

**The final logo, colors, visual branding, and brand identity are NOT settled.**

Consequences for decision-making:

- **No major strategic decision may depend on the current visual branding.**
- UX, layout, and functionality may be improved now.
- Final branding is decided later.

Treat today's visuals as provisional. Build so that final branding can be applied later without structural rework.

---

## 8. Dashboard vision

Long term, Salma has **one operational and performance dashboard** covering the website, Instagram, the newsletter, and the AI/editorial pipeline.

It should help answer:

- What was produced?
- What was published?
- What performed well?
- What did readers engage with?
- Which formats work?
- Which topics work?
- How is each channel performing?
- How healthy is the editorial pipeline?
- How is the AI performing?

Its purpose is **continuous improvement with minimal manual reporting**.

---

## 9. The decision filter

Apply this to every future product decision:

> **Does it materially improve at least one of the following?**
>
> - [ ] Reader value
> - [ ] Editorial quality
> - [ ] Clarity
> - [ ] Usefulness
> - [ ] Engagement
> - [ ] Production speed
> - [ ] Automation
> - [ ] Scalability
> - [ ] Ability to operate with minimal human labor

If nothing on this list is materially improved, **question whether it needs to be built at all.**
