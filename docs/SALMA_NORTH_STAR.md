## What this document is

This is Salma's permanent strategic direction and its set of non-negotiable principles. It describes **what Salma is trying to become and how to decide** — not what is currently built.

Every significant future decision — **product, editorial, architecture, growth, distribution, or monetization** — must first be checked against this document. If a proposal conflicts with a principle here, the proposal changes, or this document is deliberately and explicitly revised. It is not overridden silently.

This document does **not** track execution status. Status, priorities, verified production state, and postponed items live in the Master Roadmap. North Star = direction and principles. Roadmap = execution and status.

---

## 1. Core ambition

Salma is **not merely a health-news website**.

Its long-term ambition is to become **the primary trusted source for health and medical news in Kuwait, and later the GCC** — a source that:

- readers trust
- doctors follow
- hospitals monitor
- healthcare executives pay attention to
- other media reference

Every strategic choice is ultimately judged against this ambition.

---

## 2. Instagram-first distribution

Salma's website is important as:

- the authoritative source
- the archive
- the searchable knowledge base
- the destination for deeper reading

But the **primary distribution and growth engine is Instagram first**, with X/Twitter as a secondary channel where appropriate.

Product and editorial decisions must therefore ask:

> **Can this become a compelling, highly shareable Instagram post / carousel / story?**

Web-first thinking alone is not sufficient.

### Relationship to the three primary products

Salma remains focused on three serious standalone editorial products — **Website, Instagram, Newsletter** — each judged on its own editorial merit, none a mechanical copy of another. This section refines that principle with a distribution hierarchy: the website holds the authoritative record; **Instagram is where Salma grows**; the newsletter is the curated relationship channel.

---

## 3. Content-to-influence strategy

Salma uses valuable health content to build **genuine relationships and influence within Kuwait's healthcare sector**.

Key audiences:

- doctors
- hospital owners
- healthcare executives
- private hospitals
- clinics
- regulators
- insurers
- health-tech companies
- pharma/medtech stakeholders

The platform should gradually become **part of the daily professional information environment of the healthcare sector** — not only a consumer destination.

---

## 4. Doctor-transfer growth loop

Doctor transfers are **strategically important, not merely a content category**.

When Salma reports that a doctor moved from one hospital/clinic to another:

- the doctor is likely to repost it
- the destination hospital may repost it
- colleagues, patients, friends and family may share it
- the story reaches new professional and patient audiences
- doctors begin following Salma to see who is moving where

This creates a **natural audience-growth and relationship loop**. Doctor-transfer coverage must therefore be:

- verified
- visually strong
- respectful
- concise
- optimized for social sharing

---

## 5. Doctor research → public news

Salma builds a strong editorial format around **doctors' peer-reviewed research**: when a Kuwait/GCC doctor publishes in a reputable scientific journal, Salma transforms it into accessible public-facing news.

Reference format:

- doctor photo
- doctor name and institution
- simple headline
- what the study examined
- main result
- why it matters to ordinary people
- journal/publication details

Priority goes to research with broad public interest, including: obesity, diabetes, pregnancy and childbirth, pediatrics, dermatology, aesthetics, cancer, cardiovascular health, mental wellbeing, nutrition, longevity, prevention.

This creates value in both directions:

- **for the public** — science translated into understandable information
- **for doctors** — legitimate scientific work gains broader visibility

Over time, Salma should become **a desirable place for doctors to have their work featured**.

## 6. Prestige and recognition

Salma builds editorial formats that create **professional prestige**:

- Doctor of the Month
- physician profiles
- research highlights
- major career milestones
- leadership appointments
- notable achievements

The goal: high-quality Salma coverage becomes **professionally desirable and aspirational** among healthcare professionals.

**Recognition must remain editorially credible and must never become automatic pay-to-play.**

---

## 7. Commercial objective

If Salma becomes the trusted and widely followed healthcare media source, it should naturally become **a leading advertising and reputation platform** for:

- doctors
- hospitals
- clinics
- healthcare services
- pharma/medtech
- insurers
- health-tech companies

Potential commercial products may include:

- clearly labeled sponsored content
- promoted doctor profiles
- hospital/service campaigns
- event partnerships
- newsletter sponsorship
- social media packages

**Editorial and commercial content must always remain clearly distinguishable.**

---

## 8. Trust is the strategic asset

Salma's influence depends on trust. Therefore:

- factual accuracy comes first
- source quality matters
- paid content must be labeled
- editorial coverage cannot simply be bought
- doctors and hospitals should value Salma **because appearing there means something**

**If commercial activity damages editorial credibility, it damages the entire strategy.** Trust outranks revenue in every conflict between the two.

---

## 9. Editorial differentiation — "After the News"

Salma does not only say **what happened**. It increasingly explains:

- why it matters
- what is new
- what the evidence says
- what remains uncertain
- what it means for Kuwait/GCC

This is especially important for: Develop Story, scientific research, regulation, healthcare economics, and global health trends.

"After the News" remains a **core Salma editorial identity principle**, not an optional format. Salma answers three questions, not one:

1. **What happened?**
2. **What does it mean?**
3. **What is behind / after the news?**

### The depth ladder

Depth scales with the importance of the topic:

**short explanation → deeper analysis → investigative / research material**

Not every story earns the top rung. Important stories must not be left on the bottom one.

## 11. Distribution lens

For every major editorial format, always consider **both**:

- **Website value** — the authoritative full record
- **Instagram/social value** — the strongest possible social translation of it

A format that works only on the web is incomplete.

---

## 12. Product decision test

Before building a major feature, ask:

1. Does this help Salma become a more important source of health news?
2. Does it create content people will genuinely share or follow?
3. Does it strengthen Salma's relationships/influence in healthcare?
4. Does it improve trust or authority?
5. Can it contribute to future sustainable monetization without damaging credibility?

**If a feature serves none of these, it is probably not a priority.**

### Operational quality filter (retained from V1)

A proposal that passes the strategic test should also materially improve at least one of:

- [ ] Reader value
- [ ] Editorial quality
- [ ] Clarity
- [ ] Usefulness
- [ ] Engagement
- [ ] Production speed
- [ ] Automation
- [ ] Scalability
- [ ] Ability to operate with minimal human labor

If nothing on either list is materially improved, **question whether it needs to be built at all.**

## 13. Near-term strategic priority

The near-term product emphasis shifts toward:

> **content formats + Instagram/social distribution + healthcare-network effects**

rather than adding website features for their own sake. The website remains important, but **the roadmap must not treat web traffic as the only measure of success.**

---

## 14. Product quality standard *(retained from V1)*

Everything Salma produces aims to be: highly useful, accurate, clean, concise, elegant, easy to consume, visually and editorially attractive, respectful of the reader's time — and free from filler, unnecessary repetition, and generic or low-value content.

**The goal of any Salma output is a compact, valuable, intelligent dose of information.** The benchmark is global-quality editorial and product standards — not local or category-average standards.

---

## 15. AI-first operating model *(retained from V1)*

The long-term operating goal is **minimum human labor + maximum editorial quality** — pursued together, neither traded away.

**AI handles** as much repeatable work as can safely be automated: discovery, research, source analysis, evidence analysis, topic selection, drafting, summarization, translation, adaptation by channel, Instagram content preparation, newsletter curation, categorization and metadata, performance analysis, workflow coordination.

**Humans focus on**: important approvals, exceptional editorial judgment, sensitive or high-risk decisions, strategic decisions.

**Anti-patterns:** do not build workflows that require staff to repeatedly copy-paste, manually categorize, fill unnecessary forms, rewrite the same information once per channel, or perform repetitive administrative work. If a repetitive task can reasonably be automated, prefer automation.

---

## 16. One content intelligence, three channels *(retained from V1)*

One intelligent editorial engine feeds all products. **A topic enters the system once.** The AI system determines what it deserves: a website article, an In Brief, an After-the-News analysis, a deeper investigation, an Instagram carousel or short format, newsletter inclusion, multiple channels — or no publication at all. Each channel receives content **adapted for that medium**. "No publication" is a legitimate and expected outcome.

### Per-channel principles

- **Website**: content architecture, functionality, editorial presentation, information density, discoverability, article usefulness, reading experience, In Brief, video, research and studies, deeper analysis and After-the-News formats, comments/search/sharing where genuinely useful, mobile experience.
- **Instagram**: a primary product with an AI-driven editorial engine producing high-quality hooks, carousels, concise explainers, After-the-News analysis, study explanations, important numbers, comparisons, captions, source references — **never merely reposting website headlines**.
- **Newsletter**: a primary product, **curated, not a feed** — *"here are the few health stories actually worth your time."*

## 17. Branding status — intentionally unsettled *(retained from V1)*

**The final logo, colors, visual branding, and brand identity are NOT settled.**

- No major strategic decision may depend on the current visual branding.
- UX, layout, and functionality may be improved now.
- Final branding is decided later.

Treat today's visuals as provisional. Build so final branding can be applied later without structural rework.

---

## 18. Dashboard vision *(retained from V1)*

Long term, Salma has **one operational and performance dashboard** covering the website, Instagram, the newsletter, and the AI/editorial pipeline: what was produced, what was published, what performed, what readers engaged with, which formats and topics work, how each channel and the AI are performing. Its purpose is **continuous improvement with minimal manual reporting**.
