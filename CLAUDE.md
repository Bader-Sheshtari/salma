# Salma product strategy — read before significant decisions

Before making ANY major product, editorial, growth, distribution, monetization, architecture, or roadmap decision, read:

- `docs/SALMA_NORTH_STAR.md` — permanent strategic direction and non-negotiable principles (V2, 2026-10-15): core ambition (primary trusted health-news source for Kuwait → GCC); Instagram-first distribution with the website as authoritative record; content-to-influence strategy in the healthcare sector; doctor-transfer growth loop; doctor-research format; prestige formats; commercial objective with trust as the strategic asset; "After the News" editorial identity; strategic content pillars; the product decision test; AI-first operation; branding intentionally unsettled.
- `docs/SALMA_MASTER_ROADMAP.md` — canonical execution status and planned work.

Rules:
- Major proposals must first be checked against the North Star: pass the product decision test (§12) and align with the core ambition, Instagram-first distribution, and trust principles.
- Trust outranks revenue; editorial and commercial content stay clearly distinguishable; recognition formats are never pay-to-play.
- Existing roadmap items must NOT be silently removed or cancelled; reorganize or re-status them explicitly instead.
- Final branding (logo, colors, visual identity) is intentionally not settled — do not base strategic decisions on current visual branding.
- North Star revisions are deliberate and explicit (dated at the top), never implicit.
