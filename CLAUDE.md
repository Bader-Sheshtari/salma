# Salma product strategy — read before significant decisions

Before making significant product, editorial, architecture, or roadmap decisions, read:

- `docs/SALMA_NORTH_STAR.md` — product direction and non-negotiable principles (three primary products: Website, Instagram, Newsletter; AI-first operation; "After the News" editorial identity; decision filter).
- `docs/SALMA_MASTER_ROADMAP.md` — canonical execution status and planned work.

Rules:
- Major proposals must align with the North Star and pass its decision filter.
- Existing roadmap items must NOT be silently removed or cancelled; reorganize or re-status them explicitly instead.
- Final branding (logo, colors, visual identity) is intentionally not settled — do not base strategic decisions on current visual branding.
